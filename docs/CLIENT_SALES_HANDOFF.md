# Client Sales Handoff

The client sales handoff layer turns the commercial package into buyer-facing sales material.

It includes:

- sales narrative,
- demo agenda,
- follow-up email,
- buyer FAQ,
- sales handoff package.

## Purpose

The layer helps explain the FinanceOps Agent to a CFO, controller, founder, or finance operations lead without overstating production readiness.

It makes the project easier to present by separating:

- what the agent does,
- why it matters,
- what proof points to show,
- what claims are safe,
- what claims must be avoided,
- what the buyer should do next.

## Key rule

Sales handoff must not claim the project is production-ready, payment-ready, or ROI-verified until client-owned data, mappings, approvals, and production exclusions are accepted.
